How to start learning product design as a complete beginner

Product design is designing digital products such as apps and websites so that they are useful and easy to use. It covers user research, wireframes, prototypes and visual design.

Learn the design process

Understand the steps: find out who the users are and what they need, sketch ideas, build a prototype, test it with real people and improve it.

Learn a design tool

Figma is widely used and has a free plan. Learn to build simple layouts, reusable components and clickable prototypes.

Study good design

Pick apps you use every day and note what works and what frustrates you. Learn the basics of layout, spacing, colour and readability, and why accessibility matters.

Build a portfolio

Design two or three small case studies from problem to prototype and explain your decisions. Employers hire designers for their thinking, not just their screens.
