How to start learning business analysis as a complete beginner

Business analysis is the work of understanding what a business needs and turning it into clear requirements that a team can build. Business analysts sit between the business side and the technical team, so communication matters as much as any tool.

Learn how businesses work

Understand the basics: how companies make money, how departments such as sales, finance and operations fit together, and what a business process is. Read case studies and company reports in industries that interest you.

Learn the core skills

Practise gathering requirements by asking good questions, writing clear user stories, drawing simple process maps and documenting what you find. Spreadsheets and basic SQL help you check the data behind a problem.

Practise on a real example

Pick a process you know, such as how a local shop takes orders. Map how it works today, where it goes wrong and how you would improve it, then write it up as a short document.
