How to start learning governance, risk and compliance (GRC) as a complete beginner

Governance, risk and compliance (GRC) is the work of making sure an organisation follows the rules that apply to it, understands its risks and manages them. GRC analysts work with policies, risk assessments, audits and standards.

Learn the vocabulary

Governance is how decisions are made and controlled, risk is the chance that something goes wrong, and compliance is meeting the rules. Learn these three ideas and how they connect.

Know the common frameworks

Read introductions to widely used standards such as ISO 27001 for information security, and to the data protection laws that apply in your country or the countries you want to work in.

Build basic technical awareness

You do not need to be a hacker, but you should understand ideas such as access control, backups and incident response.

Practise a risk assessment

Choose a small organisation, list its main information assets, the threats to each and the controls that would reduce the risk. Write it up as a short report.
