How to start learning healthcare business analysis as a complete beginner

Healthcare business analysis applies business analysis to hospitals, clinics, insurers and health technology companies. The analyst helps them improve processes and choose or build the right systems while following health rules.

Start with business analysis basics

Learn requirements gathering, process mapping and writing user stories first. These are the core skills in every business analyst job.

Learn how healthcare works

Understand patient journeys, how records and billing work, and who the key players are, such as providers, insurers and regulators. Public health agency websites are a free place to start.

Respect privacy rules

Health information is sensitive and legally protected in most countries, for example under HIPAA in the United States. Learn the principle of patient confidentiality before you work with any health data.

Practise safely

Map a simple healthcare process, such as booking a clinic appointment, using only public information. Never use real patient data.
