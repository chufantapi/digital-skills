How to start learning healthtech as a complete beginner

Healthtech is the use of technology and data in healthcare. It includes electronic health records, telemedicine, health apps and the analysis of health data to improve care. You do not need to be a doctor or nurse to work in it, but you do need to understand how health data behaves.

Learn the language of health

Start with the basic terms and how a clinic or hospital runs: patients, records, billing, lab tests and referrals. Free material from public health bodies and university open courses is enough to begin with.

Get comfortable with data basics

Many beginner-friendly healthtech roles involve working with data. Learn spreadsheets first, then SQL, in the same order as the data analytics path.

Understand privacy

Health data is some of the most sensitive data there is. Learn the idea of patient confidentiality, and read about the privacy rules that apply where you want to work, such as HIPAA in the United States or your own country's data protection law.

Build a small project

Take a public, anonymised health dataset, for example one published by a government health agency, and answer one question with it. Never use real patient records. Write up what you found in plain language.
