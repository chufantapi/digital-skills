Learning a digital skill in 2026 is one of the smartest moves many people can make.

With digital technology spreading into every industry, demand keeps growing for skilled professionals in fields such as data analysis, business analysis, data science and cybersecurity.

Take data science as an example. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics projects that employment of data scientists will grow 34 percent from 2024 to 2034, much faster than the average for all occupations.

Cybersecurity shows a similar picture. The same agency projects 29 percent growth for information security analysts over the same period. These are US figures, so treat them as a guide rather than a promise for any one country.

AI is also changing all of these roles. Learners now need to do more than master the core skills: they also need to learn how to use AI in their work, from studying to doing the job. That is where 10Alytics comes in. Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook.

What is 10Alytics
